Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information DirectionalMarker collects, how it is used, what information may be publicly visible, and the choices you have when using the website.

Information We Collect

When you create and use a DirectionalMarker account, we may collect and store the following information:

  • Name: Your name, which is used to identify your account and display your name throughout the website where appropriate.
  • Email Address: Your email address, which is used to create and manage your account and for authentication-related purposes.
  • Account Information: Information associated with your DirectionalMarker account, including your authentication status and account identifier.
  • Timetable Information: Information you enter when registering your timetable, including class codes, class names, teacher names, and room codes.
  • Usage Information: Information that may be automatically collected when you use the website, such as browser type, device information, IP address, and basic website usage information.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect may be used for the following purposes:

  • Creating and maintaining your DirectionalMarker account
  • Authenticating users and allowing them to securely sign in
  • Displaying your name when you are signed in
  • Allowing you to register and manage your school timetable
  • Displaying timetable and class information to other users
  • Allowing users to search for students and view their registered timetable
  • Allowing users to view other students who are registered in a particular class
  • Maintaining and improving DirectionalMarker
  • Communicating with users when necessary

Public Timetable and Class Information

DirectionalMarker is designed to allow students to share timetable information with other users of the website. Information that you submit as part of your timetable may therefore be publicly accessible to other DirectionalMarker users.

This may include your name, class codes, class names, teacher names, room codes, school days, and periods associated with your timetable.

Other users may be able to search for your name, view your registered timetable, and select a class to see a list of students registered in that class.

Do not enter information into your timetable that you do not want other users to be able to see.

Email Addresses

Your email address is used for account authentication and management. Your email address is not intended to be displayed as part of the publicly accessible timetable or class directory.

Firebase

DirectionalMarker uses Firebase services provided by Google to provide account authentication and store certain information required for the website to function.

Account passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are not stored directly by DirectionalMarker. DirectionalMarker does not intentionally store users' passwords in the website's database.

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information stored by DirectionalMarker. However, no website or online service can guarantee complete security of information transmitted or stored online.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

DirectionalMarker may use cookies, local storage, or similar browser technologies where necessary to maintain functionality, remember account information, or provide website features.

The website may also use third-party analytics or tracking services to understand how the website is used and improve its functionality.

Third-Party Services

DirectionalMarker may use third-party services, including Firebase and Google services, to provide website functionality. These services may process information in accordance with their own privacy policies and terms.

Data Retention

Information associated with your account may be retained for as long as your account remains active or for as long as it is required to provide DirectionalMarker's services.

Timetable information may remain available to other users while it is registered to your account. If you no longer want your timetable to be available, you should update or remove the timetable where the website provides that functionality.

Your Choices

You may choose not to provide certain information; however, some information is required to create an account or use particular features of DirectionalMarker.

You should only submit timetable information that you are comfortable sharing with other users of the website.

Children and Young People

DirectionalMarker is intended for use by students and members of their school communities. Users should follow any applicable rules or requirements set by their school, parent or guardian, or other responsible authority when using the website.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. You should check this page periodically to ensure that you are aware of any changes.
